Implementation in this repository began on August 9, 2026. Its Git commits use their real author and commit dates. No commit is backdated to manufacture contribution activity.

## Claim policy

The repository treats earlier statements as requirements to validate, not proof that they were already achieved. In particular, the 45 percent figure is published only with a committed workload, benchmark command, raw result, environment metadata, and exact statement of what was avoided.
